Priority Lexus Kiosk v0.20 — Model Discovery Data

The Find Your Lexus experience is now based on the Lexus model family rather than live inventory.
Primary current-model reference: Lexus USA 2026 lineup / Lexus model pages, researched September 2026.

Model data is intentionally stored in the `models` JavaScript array in index.html so it can be updated
without changing the presentation logic.

IMPORTANT IMAGE NOTE:
The Lexus USA Newsroom product photography page states that its downloadable materials are for editorial
use only and not for advertising/marketing. Those restricted press images were therefore not packaged
into this dealership kiosk prototype.

For production, replace the CSS vehicle-stage art with Lexus-approved dealer/OEM DAM images or another
asset source your dealership is licensed to use.
